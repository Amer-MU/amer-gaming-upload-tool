Amer Gaming Upload Tool is a personal desktop application. It uploads videos only to the YouTube channel of its owner.

What data is used

  • When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives an access token and a refresh token for the owner's YouTube channel. Google's permissions are limited to uploading videos and reading the channel's basic information.
  • The app reads the owner's own video files and text files from folders on the owner's computer.

Where data is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's computer. The app does not send them to any server of its own.

What is shared

Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties. Data goes only to Google, to upload videos and read the channel's status, as Google's API requires.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the stored tokens from the computer.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always on this page.
